# ARV Tech77 Dispute Pack Terminology Review v1

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the Dispute Pack terminology review boundary for ARV and ProofOps.

It defines which Dispute Pack-adjacent words, phrases, labels, headings, UI text, API-facing descriptions, documentation language, repository language, demo language and externally visible statements may be used by public, open-source or externally visible ARV surfaces.

This document does not authorize implementation, publication, release, deployment, packaging, repository exposure, public API exposure, official Evidence Certificate issuance, Evidence Certificate sale, Bundle Evidence Package sale, Dispute Pack sale, public certificate verification service or external distribution.

Its purpose is to prevent accidental dispute authority, accidental legal representation, accidental adjudication, accidental claims resolution authority, accidental customer-specific evidence exposure or accidental commercial entitlement through Dispute Pack terminology.

The governing principle remains:

**Proof != Dispute Authority**

## 2. Governing Doctrine

Cryptographic evidence may establish integrity, traceability, provenance and internal consistency within the scope of captured evidence.

Dispute Pack terminology may describe bounded technical organization of evidence for dispute preparation only.

Dispute Pack terminology must not create legal authority, judicial authority, attorney-client authority, representation authority, adjudicative authority, claim-resolution authority, settlement authority, commercial entitlement authority, official ARV issuance authority or official dispute authority.

The canonical local kernel remains governed by the **LOCAL_L0** isolation doctrine.

No public verifier, open-source candidate, local tool, demo, public API, public repository, public document, automated output or deployment surface may represent itself as issuing, selling, validating, approving, adjudicating, resolving or delivering an official Dispute Pack.

## 3. Current Controlled-Export Status

**NO-GO**

Any demo language must be non-commercial, non-authoritative and clearly bounded.

Proof may support a dispute.
